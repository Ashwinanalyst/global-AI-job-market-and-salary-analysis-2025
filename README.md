

📊 Global AI Job Market & Salary Trends 2025

A Data Analytics Project using Excel & Power BI

1. 📌 Project Overview & Objective

This project analyzes the Global AI Job Market using Excel and Power BI.
The dataset contains details about:

•Job titles

•Experience levels

•Employment types

•Company locations

•Salaries & benefits

•Remote work ratio

🎯 Main Objective

•To derive meaningful insights into:

•Global salary trends

•Experience-level patterns

•Remote work adoption

•Hiring trends across countries

•Top-paying job roles in AI

2. 🗂 Data Source

•Dataset: Global AI Job Market & Salary Trends 2025

•Source: Kaggle

•Domain: HR Analytics / Job Market Analysis

•Timeline: Data represents global job records collected for 2025

3. ❓ Problem Statement

•The project aims to analyze and understand:

•Global AI job market trends

•Salary distribution by job role, country, and experience

•Top-paying countries & job titles

•Impact of experience level on salary

•Relationship between job type, benefits, and remote work

•Job demand & hiring patterns across regions


4. 🛠 Tools & Technologies Used
•Excel

•Data cleaning

•Data transformation

•Pivot tables

•Calculated columns

•Power BI

•Data modeling

•DAX measures

•Interactive dashboards

•Visual analytics

5. 🧹 Data Pre-Processing (Excel / Power Query)

•Key cleaning steps:

•Removed duplicates & null values

•Standardized column names

•Converted salary columns to numeric format

•Handled missing values (Benefits Score, Remote Ratio)

•Filtered job-related categories

•Created calculated columns for salary segmentation

•Built pivot tables for initial insights

6. 🧩 Data Modeling & DAX (Power BI)
Data Model Includes:

•Fact table: Job Records

•Dimension tables: Experience, Employment Type, Country

•DAX Measures Created:

•Average Salary

•Total Jobs

•Top 10 Highest Paying Countries

•Salary by Experience Level

•Remote Work Ratio Distribution

•Benefits Score Analysis

Dashboard Includes:

•KPI Cards

•Interactive tables

•Interactive Features Used

•Drill-down

•Clean consolidated layout

9. 🔍 Insights & Findings
Descriptive Insights

•Senior-level AI roles receive the highest salary range

•Remote job opportunities increased significantly

•Certain countries dominate AI job salaries

•Diagnostic Insights

•Salary differences heavily depend on job title & experience

•Companies offering higher benefit scores attract senior talent

•Predictive Insights

•Hiring demand is expected to grow in data science & ML engineering

•Remote work is predicted to continue rising

•Prescriptive Insights

•Job seekers should focus on skill areas with highest salary growth

•Companies should optimize benefits and remote policies to attract talent

10. 🏁 Conclusion

•Using Excel and Power BI, this project successfully uncovered global AI job trends, salary patterns, and hiring behaviors.
The findings help:

•Job seekers understand high-demand skills & salary expectations

•Companies benchmark salaries and benefits

•Analysts identify data-driven workforce trends
